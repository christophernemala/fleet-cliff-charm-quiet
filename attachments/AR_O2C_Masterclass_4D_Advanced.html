MCP, Context Windows & Cloud Architecture

Understanding Model Context Protocol, LLM capabilities, and deployment models for enterprise AR automation

ℹ️
What is MCP (Model Context Protocol)?
MCP is an open standard (pioneered by Anthropic) that provides a universal protocol for connecting AI models to external data sources and tools. Think of it as USB-C for AI — a single standardized interface that lets your AI agents securely access ERP systems, bank feeds, CRM data, and document repositories without custom integration code for each. In AR automation, MCP enables your Collection Agent to pull real-time aging data from Oracle, your Payment Matching Agent to access bank transaction feeds, and your Voice Agent to retrieve customer communication history — all through a unified, secure protocol layer.

For UAE-based enterprises, Azure UAE North (Dubai) region provides the best combination of AI service availability and data residency compliance. ⚠️
UAE Regulatory Considerations
Voice AI for collections in the UAE must comply with TDRA (Telecommunications & Digital Government Regulatory Authority) regulations. Key requirements: (1) AI must identify itself as automated at the start of every call, (2) calls must comply with do-not-call timings (no calls before 8 AM or after 9 PM GST), (3) recordings must be stored for 2 years per Central Bank guidelines, (4) Arabic language support is mandatory for government-related collections, and (5) personal data handling must align with UAE Federal Data Protection Law (2021). Always engage your Legal/Compliance team before deploying voice agents.
💡
Real-World Results from Voice AI in Collections
Organizations deploying AI voice agents for AR collections report: 70% automation rate on outbound collection calls (only 30% require human escalation), 35% improvement in First Call Resolution (FCR), 25% reduction in DSO within the first 90 days, and 60% cost reduction compared to traditional call center operations. The key success factor is integrating the voice agent directly with the ERP system so it has real-time account context during conversations.

How the AI Credit Scoring Agent Works
The Credit Scoring Agent (Bot #4) uses a gradient-boosted ML model trained on historical payment data to calculate a composite score (0-100) based on: payment history (35%) — average days to pay, late payment frequency, broken promises; financial health (25%) — D&B rating, financial statements, industry risk; relationship tenure (20%) — years as customer, order volume trend, dispute frequency; and external signals (20%) — news sentiment, market conditions, peer comparisons. The model re-scores all customers weekly and auto-adjusts tiers when scores cross thresholds. Tier changes trigger notifications to the Credit Manager and update ERP credit limits via API.
